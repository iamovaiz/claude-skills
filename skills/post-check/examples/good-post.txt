Last month a client asked why their order sheet took three days to approve.

We traced it. Four people signed the same form, and two of them only checked the total.

So we cut it to two signatures and put the total on page one. Approval now takes an afternoon.

The fix wasn't software. It was asking who actually reads the form, and why.

If your approvals crawl, list every signature and write next to each one what that person checks. Cross out the ones that check nothing new.

What's the slowest form in your team right now?
