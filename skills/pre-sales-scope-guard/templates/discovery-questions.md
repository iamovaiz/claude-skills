# Discovery questions

Ask only what the prospect hasn't already answered. One conversation, 30 to 45 minutes.

## The problem
1. Walk me through how this works today, step by step. Who does each step?
2. Where does it go wrong, and how often?
3. What does that cost you: time, money, customers, mistakes?
4. What have you already tried?

## Success
5. If this works, what number changes, and by how much?
6. How will you know in 3 months that it was worth it?

## Money and decisions
7. Have you set aside a budget for this, even a rough range?
8. Who else needs to agree before you go ahead?
9. When do you need it working, and what happens if it's later?

## For commission or revenue-share deals
10. What is your commission or margin on a typical sale?
11. What do you keep after costs?
12. How many sales did you make last month, and where did they come from?

## Constraints
13. What systems, hardware or suppliers does this have to work with?
14. Who will use it every day, and how comfortable are they with new tools?
