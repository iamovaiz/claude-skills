# Paid discovery offer (template)

Fill the brackets with real figures from the user. Never invent the price.

---

Hi [name],

Thanks for walking me through [the problem in their words].

Before I can give you a price I'd stand behind, I need to see how [the process] works in practice. So I'd suggest a short paid discovery first:

- **What I'll do:** [e.g. two sessions with your team, a review of your current sheets and system, a written map of where time and money are lost]
- **What you get:** [e.g. a written report with 2 or 3 options, each with a price range, a timeline and what it doesn't include]
- **Time:** [e.g. 2 weeks]
- **Fee:** [amount]. If you go ahead with the build within [e.g. 30 days], this fee comes off the build price.

If you'd rather not, that's fine. I can still send a short outline of the options as I see them now, with the price ranges marked as estimates.

[sign-off]
